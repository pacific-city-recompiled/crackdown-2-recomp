# Crackdown 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Checks are sent when you collect agility orbs, hidden orbs (optional) and audio logs (optional). Your Progressive
Skills (Agility, Driving, Explosives, Strength, Firearms) come from the multiworld instead of from orbs and
experience: gameplay XP is turned off on a randomized save.

## What is the goal?

Reach the top level in all five skills. A Project Sunburst goal (the final phase at Agency Tower) is planned.

## What does another world's item look like in Crackdown 2?

Orbs and audio logs look and behave as normal; the check is sent when you collect them. Received skills apply
straight away.

## Which version of the game is supported?

Only the Crackdown 2 Recomp PC port with title update 0.0.5.1, installed from your own dump. Original hardware
and emulators are not supported.
